LOGO SIZING
Every mark is scaled to the same optical size: its ink area is matched to the
Google logo it replaces (98,365px on the flat templates, 212,452px on the
standard angled), then contain-clamped so it never exceeds the master's logo
footprint. All marks share the master's centre point and aspect ratios are
preserved. Wide wordmarks (Nextdoor, Trulia) hit the width clamp before
reaching the target area — replace with icon-form assets for full uniformity.

TYPOGRAPHY
Hind Bold, matched to the masters. Flat CTA cap 37px on baseline y=1203;
standard-angled CTA cap 46px on baseline y=1090. Where a platform name pushed
the line past the masters' 1080px print-safe width, cap size was reduced only
enough to fit — never enlarged.
