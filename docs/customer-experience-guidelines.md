# Customer experience guidelines

Use Semrush Local as the reference for compact task-focused customer workflows. Inspect the user's open reference when available; supplied Semrush screenshots are a fallback, not evidence of a fresh live inspection.

- Lead with the selected location, relevant results, and the next action.
- Keep reviews in short rows; open full reviews and drafts in a dialog.
- Put occasional configuration in Settings. Put explanations and evidence in accessible expandable help, usable by keyboard and touch.
- Keep infrastructure names, API identifiers, provider budgets, and implementation details out of routine customer controls. Retain appropriate disclosures in the privacy policy and keep AI processing consent explicit.
- Show actionable errors, unavailable data, and outdated scan warnings. Do not hide meaningful problems to make a screen appear cleaner.
- Show scan estimates before approval. Keep no-charge testing explicit; do not activate pricing through a copy or layout update.
- Keep customer locations separate. Preserve account access rules and the selected location across pages.
- Replies and profile changes remain drafts. Approval must not publish. Do not add publishing actions to customer pages.
- Directory tracking must distinguish manual checks from connected distribution. Never imply a directory is connected merely because it appears in the catalog.
- Keep order management available and separate from marketing tools. Stand-only access must not expose marketing data.

For UI verification, inspect the eight customer navigation pages, plus relevant dialogs and a narrow viewport. Save screenshots and state any unavailable surfaces honestly.
