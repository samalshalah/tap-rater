# Content Phase 3: Images and Listing Templates

Date: 2026-09-10. Scope: the third phase of the content/design correction plan.

## Changes

- Product cards share the existing catalog treatment: 8px corners, square uncropped image frames, naturally wrapping titles, and prices aligned at the bottom. Category, solution, related-product, and compatible-product grids use two mobile columns, three at large widths, and four at extra-large widths. Single-product categories no longer enlarge one card to 600px.
- Branded-filter listings identify Standard photos as "Standard design shown" alongside the Branded price. Purchase eligibility, prices, quantities, inventory, and checkout behavior are unchanged.
- Product galleries include saved photos, deduplicate URLs, exclude known production-template files, and label Branded angled templates as layout examples. The selected thumbnail exposes its pressed state. The scroll track keeps all thumbnails reachable.
- The Multi-Link example now matches the stand and phone. It is labeled illustrative and is not a print file, QR-scanning proof, customer testimonial, or evidence of a shipped product.
- Homepage, Multi-Link service, and Multi-Link category defaults reference the same optimized example. The homepage no longer adds a second HTML phone over the combined image. Merchant-supplied replacement images do not inherit the example caption.
- The public category name remains "Multi-Link Stand"; the service page uses "Multi-Link". Repeated category introductions are suppressed without discarding additional merchant paragraphs.
- The exact observed legacy Yelp description no longer implies Standard includes QR. Unrelated merchant-authored copy is preserved.
