## Delivered

- Populated Appointments with the existing Connect With Us and Explore Our Services products as secondary uses. Populated Feedback with Rate Your Experience even when its primary admin category is Reviews. Primary product records, artwork, and pricing were not changed.
- Added honest category copy: stands open the merchant's existing booking provider, form, or survey; they do not provide a booking engine or automatically submit feedback.
- Permanently redirected `/category/custom-stands` to `/custom-stands`, and consolidated the website-link category aliases. Updated category links to the canonical paths.
- Restricted sitemap categories to enabled, populated collections. Empty or disabled category metadata is noindex; disabled categories remain unavailable. Added crawlable collection links to the shop and pricing links from Multi-Link and the restored helper.
- Added Standard and Branded Product variants with actual active-option prices, distinct checkout SKUs, matching gallery images, and directly selectable `?design=standard` / `?design=branded` URLs. Canonicals remain the base product URL. Default purchasable size/color adjustments match the chooser, schema, and server-rendered headline price. No subscription, disabled, or quote-only offer is invented.
- Added product/category breadcrumbs. The customization distinction is described as a Design property, without inventing a Google-supported `variesBy` value.
- Restored `/review-links-generator` as a working public helper using the existing server-side Google business-search endpoint, with manual-link validation, copy/open controls, and a clipboard fallback. No automatic review submission or rating claim.
- Marked the setup/change-link utility forms noindex, follow. They remain usable and are not blocked from crawlers reading the directive.
- Removed automatic mid-word ellipsis truncation in product metadata. Complete admin-authored metadata is preserved; generated copy prefers whole title segments/sentences.
- Preserved homepage layout, card styles, product galleries, source product images, checkout/payment configuration, and customer data.
